SafeNote Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026
Publisher: Michael Ruggirello
Contact: safenoteappai@gmail.com

The short version

SafeNote keeps your sessions on your phone. We do not collect, receive, store or sell your recordings, transcripts, notes or any other information about you or your clients. We have no servers that receive your data, no accounts and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in SafeNote stays on your device:

  • Audio recordings of sessions and dictation
  • Transcripts
  • Progress notes and any psychotherapy notes
  • Your passphrase and settings

Recording, transcription and note writing are done by software running on your phone. Notes and transcripts are stored in a database encrypted with your passphrase, which only you know. We cannot read your data and cannot recover your passphrase. If you forget it, your notes cannot be recovered.

Recordings are deleted from your device when you approve a note. If you delete a session, its data is deleted from your device.

What we collect

Nothing. SafeNote has no account system, no cloud sync, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. We do not receive any information from the app.

The only time the app uses the internet

The first time you set up SafeNote, it downloads the speech and writing models it needs from a public model host (Hugging Face). This is a one-time download of the model files. The request carries none of your recordings, transcripts, notes or personal information. The host that serves the files may see ordinary network information such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. After the models are downloaded, SafeNote works without an internet connection.

Face ID and the microphone

  • Microphone: used only to record sessions and dictation on your device. Audio never leaves the device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: used only to unlock the app on your device. SafeNote does not receive your biometric data.

Exporting your notes

If you export or share a note (for example as text or a Word document), SafeNote hands the file to the app or destination you choose. What happens to it after that is governed by that destination, not by SafeNote. Psychotherapy notes are stored separately and are never included in exports.

Your responsibilities as a clinician

SafeNote is a drafting tool. Every note it writes is a draft that you must review and approve. You are responsible for the accuracy of your records and for meeting your professional, legal and licensing obligations, including obtaining client consent to recording where required. SafeNote is not a substitute for clinical judgment.

Children

SafeNote is a professional tool for clinicians and is not directed to children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version at this address and update the effective date.
