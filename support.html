Contact: safenoteappai@gmail.com
Please do not send recordings, transcripts, notes or client information by email. SafeNote never receives them, and we do not need them to help you.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your phone. SafeNote has no account, cloud or servers that receive your sessions.

I forgot my passphrase. It cannot be recovered. Your notes are encrypted with it and we do not have a copy.

Does it need the internet? Only once, to download the speech and writing models during setup. After that it works offline.

Setup says the download is large. The models are several gigabytes in total. Use Wi-Fi and keep the app open until the download finishes.

Writing a note is slow or the phone gets warm. Everything runs on the phone. Longer sessions take longer, and newer iPhones are faster. Keep the app open while a note is being written.

The note is wrong or missing something. Notes are drafts. Review and edit every note before approving. Use "Check sources" to see where each sentence came from in the transcript, and "Regenerate note" to try again.

How do I export a note? Open the session and choose copy, text or Word document. Psychotherapy notes are never exported.

How do I delete a session? Open the session and choose Delete session. This removes it from your device.

Reporting a problem

Email safenoteappai@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, the SafeNote version (Settings, About), and what happened. Describe the problem without including any client information.
